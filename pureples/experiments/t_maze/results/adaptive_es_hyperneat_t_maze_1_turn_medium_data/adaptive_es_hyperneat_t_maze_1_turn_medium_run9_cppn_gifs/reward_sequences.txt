Maze length: 1

One row per deployment, one symbol per episode, in order:
  rewards      H = high reward (1.0), L = low reward (0.1), - = no arm reached (episode timed out)
  chosen arm   l = left arm, r = right arm, . = no arm reached
  high arm     the arm the high reward sat on that episode; it flips at
               the deployment's switch episode(s), which is the change
               the agent has to detect from its reward feedback
  steps        how long the deployment ran, which is also how many
               frames its gif has

Fitness is the deployment's score as the test counts it: a reward only
counts inside a run of two or more consecutive episodes of the same
reward (2.0 per high episode, 1.0 per low), and the whole deployment is
zeroed if the agent only ever reached one arm.
